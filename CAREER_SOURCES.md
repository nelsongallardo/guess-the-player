# Career research & sources

Snapshot checked **11 September 2026** and subsequently re-audited in [DATA_AUDIT.md](DATA_AUDIT.md). Exactly 30 selected players: 15 Europe and 15 South America. All player objects and the matching ordered crest URL arrays are fully written out in `index.html`.

## Scope and evidence

The timeline follows senior club playing spells, including competitive reserve sides in the adult league pyramid, loans and actual playing returns. Country is the senior national team represented; broad positions allow for a player’s multiple roles. See `research/data-policy.md` for the treatment of overlapping reserve seasons and exceptional registrations.

The source snapshot is not a promise of perpetual or absolute infallibility. Sources were cross-checked rather than blindly copied: managerial jobs, youth teams, contract-only “returns”, and incomplete retirement summaries were excluded. The sources below allow every selected career to be audited.

### Cristiano Ronaldo

**Portugal · Europe · Forward.** Sporting CP (2002–2003) → Sporting CP B (2002–2003) → Manchester United (2003–2009) → Real Madrid (2009–2018) → Juventus (2018–2021) → Manchester United (2021–2022) → Al-Nassr (2023–present).[1][2]

First team precedes B team by competitive debut, contrary to the conventional infobox ordering. These are overlapping 2002/03 spells, not a claim of a permanent demotion. Andorinha and Nacional were youth-only. Current Al-Nassr status corroborated on the Saudi Pro League website.[1][2]

- **Sporting CP (2002–2003):** First competitive senior appearance: 14 August 2002, versus Inter; overlaps B-team games.
- **Sporting CP B (2002–2003):** Senior third-tier reserve side; first B appearance 1 September 2002. Overlaps Sporting CP, not a transfer away.
- **Manchester United (2021–2022):** Second playing spell.
- **Al-Nassr (2023–present):** Signed December 2022; competitive playing spell began January 2023. Current SPL player profile checked.
