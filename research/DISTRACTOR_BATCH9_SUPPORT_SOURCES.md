# Batch 9 support-profile evidence

## João Pinto

### Scope decisions and uncertainties

The Atlético Madrid period was played for adult-pyramid reserve Atlético Madrileño and is included. The three Boavista periods are separate senior playing spells. Youth teams, national teams and post-playing work are excluded. The profile remains bank-only and exists to preserve the four-contemporary origin/era requirement after Fernando Couto became playable.
