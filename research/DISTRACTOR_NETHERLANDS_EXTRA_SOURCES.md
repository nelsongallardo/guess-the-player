# Netherlands-start extra contemporaries — source audit

Exactly 5 wrong-answer-bank records. Country denotes senior national-team identity; system denotes the first included professional club’s domestic system. Ordered routes retain actual playing loans, returns and professional comebacks. No playable-roster or gameplay files changed.

## Scope and method

Wikipedia senior infoboxes were retrieved with the web extractor (Winter, Ronald de Boer) or live browser DOM (Overmars, Kuyt, Nigel de Jong). Independent National Football Teams HTML was retrieved directly and parsed with actual club-name cells preserved. Table excerpts preserve source text with whitespace normalized and cell separators inserted; Wikipedia browser excerpts retain tabs. Each source excerpt is literal retrieved text rather than a paraphrased biography.

The professional-career policy excludes both Kuyt’s initial amateur Quick Boys spell and his post-retirement comeback; the initial senior amateur appearances are explicitly acknowledged, not mislabeled youth. Winter’s 2003 endpoint is retirement/registration, not a new playing-return node. Overmars starts in calendar 1991, not the first year of the 1990/91 season.
