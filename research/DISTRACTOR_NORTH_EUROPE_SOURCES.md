# Nordic / Czech distractor-only evidence

Research snapshot: 12 September 2026. **15 new profiles: Sweden 3, Norway 4, Iceland 4, Czechia 4.** These are wrong-answer candidates, not playable targets. No crests or translation records are required. The explicit mid-task coverage correction from 14 to 15 is applied.

## Scope and verification

Each selected person has retrieved Wikipedia senior-career evidence and a separately retrieved independent domain with literal excerpts. Wikipedia was read in the real browser because the text extractor omitted critical career rows; National Football Teams (NFT) was likewise read from the real DOM so club-name columns survived. The ledger-generated source section preserves literal evidence. Counts, identifiers, required fields, nonempty routes, independent-domain coverage and non-overlap with verified-players.json are checked programmatically. These checks establish artifact consistency, not infallible history.

`country` denotes senior national-team identity; `system` denotes the first included adult domestic football system, in lowercase; `region` is Europe. Czech clubs in Czechoslovak-era leagues map to czechia. Broad role labels are Forward / Midfielder / Defender. Dates are playing-stint/retirement endpoints, not exact match dates. Lower-league developmental senior spells are distinguished from youth and from excluded post-retirement recreational football. The professional-versus-developmental boundaries are disclosed per person rather than silently treating all early lower-league years as fully professional employment.

## Candidate choices and remaining limits

- **Johan Elmander instead of Anders Svensson:** the retrieved Svensson pages conflict on Hestrafors youth versus senior play. Elmander's Holmalund senior start is consistent in both retrieved tables.
- **Eirik Bakke and Vegard Heggem instead of Morten Gamst Pedersen and Tore André Flo:** retired contemporary snapshots avoid a moving active-career endpoint for Pedersen; Flo has an unresolved Stryn youth/senior boundary. Iversen is retained only after the first adult reserve season was corroborated and added ahead of Rosenborg.
- **Patrik Berger instead of Pavel Nedvěd:** Nedvěd's zero-appearance Plzeň row overlaps Dukla and a later unused Košice registration; the first-playing-club/date question was not resolved. Berger has clear Slavia professional-contract evidence.
- Henning Berg, Brede Hangeland, Claus Lundekvam and Stig Inge Bjørnebye were also screened but not used: their retrieved tables exposed additional early-club/reserve or year conflicts. Their raw research is not part of this curated bank.
- Retained qualifications are explicit below: reserve timing (Iversen/Bakke), partially independently covered Icelandic first/last seasons, Heiðar's early reserve/Þróttur boundary, and Koller's pre-professional Milevsko classification. No exact match date, missing appearance count or full-career second-source coverage is invented.

Czech midfielder Vladimir Smicer endured a stop-start period with Liverpool but undoubtedly departed the club a hero for his exploits as a substitute in the 2005 Champions League final.

The attacking midfielder, who was capable of playing on either wing or in a central creative position, was recruited by Gerard Houllier during the summer of 1999 partly to fill the void left by Steve McManaman's exit."
