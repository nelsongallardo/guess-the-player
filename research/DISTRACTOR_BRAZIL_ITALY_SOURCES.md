# Brazil and Italy contemporary distractor-only research

Retrieved 2026-09-12. Exactly **8** wrong-answer-only profiles: **4** Pelé-era Brazil starts, **2** Neymar-era Brazil starts, **2** Italy starts. No playable/runtime roster changes. Country is senior national-team identity; system and region follow the first professional senior club. Broad roles use Defender/Midfielder/Forward. Years follow actual dated senior debuts where established rather than youth or ownership-only membership.

## Research decisions and limitations

The example names were optional. Nílton Santos, Djalma Santos, Zito and Pepe were selected for the older Brazil cohort. Garrincha was withheld because the retrieved senior table includes Serrano and Sacrofano entries whose professional scope was not resolved.[1] Vavá was withheld because the Wikipedia and independent candidate search disagreed over late club identities.[3] Didi was withheld after a secondary career table introduced pre-Madureira rows and a possible extra São Paulo spell, rather than guessing a complete route.[2][21]

Each retained person has a retrieved Wikipedia biography and at least one separately retrieved independent domain. This is not a claim that both endpoints and every club were independently confirmed twice. Djalma Santos’s and Del Piero’s final years rely on Wikipedia; their second sources corroborate earlier senior play.[7][10] Active/free-agent endpoints are latest observed playing years, not retirement.[5][6] Wikipedia infobox labels differ from actual calendar debut years for Coutinho, Del Piero and Baggio; individual notes explain the policy decisions.[5][7][8]
