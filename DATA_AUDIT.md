# Full roster re-audit — 11 September 2026

## Result and limits

All **30 unique players** were rechecked against freshly retrieved external career records, with at least two distinct source URLs per player and independent corroboration. This is a historical-data audit, not a claim that passing software tests proves the facts. The original requested scope remains **professional senior clubs**, including reserve sides competing in the adult league pyramid; youth teams, closed reserve leagues, amateur post-retirement football and coaching jobs are excluded.

The audit found a calendar-year correction for Lampard’s Swansea spell and overconfident completed-registration/ongoing-playing wording for Ronaldinho. Both are corrected. No additional in-scope professional club was established for Zanetti or the other listed players. Several date and classification qualifications remain explicit below; “rechecked” does **not** mean every historical day or uncertain registration is proven.

The app now labels the timeline **SENIOR CLUB CAREER / CARRERA SÉNIOR**, and both rule sets explicitly exclude youth teams. All ordered club-name sequences were preserved; the evidence did not justify adding a youth club to Zanetti’s career.

## Remaining evidence boundaries

- Zidane: exact reserve appearance dates and 1989/90 appearances are not established. The displayed season range includes explicitly labelled membership evidence.
- Ronaldinho: announced Ravenna signing is supported; completed federation registration and competitive return are not verified. It remains a clearly tagged signing exception, not a verified match span.
- Date ranges are career-stint labels, not a match-by-match calendar. Endpoints can represent departure or retirement after the last match; Neymar, Rooney, Tevez and Riquelme have explicit distinctions.
- Roberto Carlos: the friendly-only Atlético Mineiro tour loan is an explicitly marked exception, not competitive league football.
